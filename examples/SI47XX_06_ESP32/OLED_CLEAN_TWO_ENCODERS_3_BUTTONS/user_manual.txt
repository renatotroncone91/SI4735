OLED_CLEAN_TWO_ENCODERS_3_BUTTONS
================================

Hardware
--------
ESP32 + SI4735 + OLED 128x64 (I2C) + 2 encoders + 3 buttons

Pinout (as used in the sketch)
------------------------------
RESET_PIN 12

Encoder 1 (tuning)
- A: GPIO26
- B: GPIO27
- Push: GPIO14

Encoder 2 (volume)
- A: GPIO25
- B: GPIO33
- Push: GPIO23

I2C OLED
- SDA: GPIO21
- SCL: GPIO22

Buttons (internal pull-ups)
- MODE: GPIO13
- BAND: GPIO32
- SEEK: GPIO15

Controls
--------
Encoder 1 (tuning)
- Rotate: tune frequency
- Short press: change tuning step (FM: 5/10/20 kHz, AM/SSB: 1/5/9/10/50/100 kHz)
- Long press: change bandwidth (FM/AM/SSB)

Encoder 2 (volume)
- Rotate: volume up/down
- Short press: mute/unmute

MODE button
- Cycle FM -> AM -> SSB

BAND button
- Cycle bands when in AM/SSB (MW/LW/SW list)
- In FM mode, cycles FM entries (if more than one FM band is defined)

SEEK button (suggested function)
- FM/AM: short press seek up, long press seek down
- SSB: short press toggle USB/LSB, long press toggle BFO control

Notes
-----
- The sketch uses the SI4735 SSB patch. Make sure patch_init.h is available in your
  Arduino libraries (it is included with the PU2CLR SI4735 library examples).
- If you want a different band plan, edit bandTable[] in the sketch.
